If a new site has no visitors, the usual reflex is to improve the pages. Better headings, better copy, more links. But a search engine can't rank a page it has never read. The first question is always: has it been indexed at all?

Type your domain below. This builds the exact checks to run, in order, with the right links already filled in. Nothing is sent anywhere; it only assembles links from what you type.

Just the domain, with or without https://.

    Reading the result

    Zero results from the site: search is the clearest signal: the engine has not crawled a single page yet. A handful of results on a site with hundreds of pages means the same problem, just partial.

    If it's been more than two weeks since you submitted a sitemap and verified the property, and the count is still zero, something is blocking the crawler: a noindex tag left over from staging, a robots.txt disallow, or a password wall. Check those before anything else.
